## 💡 How to Use the Application

### Step 1: Open the Application
After installation, find the application in your start menu or Applications folder. Launch it to start creating.

### Step 2: Start a New Project
Click on "New Project" to begin writing. You can choose a genre that you prefer, such as romance, fantasy, or mystery.

### Step 3: Generate Chapters
The AI will prompt you to set the story's tone and style. Then, click on "Generate Chapters." The application will take a moment to create engaging content for you.

### Step 4: Review and Edit
Once the chapters are generated, read through them. You may edit the text as needed. The AI-generated content serves as a starting point, so feel free to make it your own.

### Step 5: Export Your Novel
When you’re satisfied, click "Export." Choose your preferred format, such as PDF or Word. Your novel is now ready to share or print!
